## Why these weights (early-tilt blend, 50-agent consensus)

early. Physical AI leads at 16 (huge and still early). Semis held at 12,
deliberately below its full 2045 size since it has already run hard, to top up
later. Grid, India and gene editing anchor at 10-11. Uranium, cyber, Bitcoin and

## The caveat to watch

MSTR (8%) is a leveraged single stock. MP and Lynas are single names too.
Combined single-stock weight ~17%. This is the price of the convexity. MSTR is at
the upper end of sensible for a leveraged single name; if risk appetite ever
drops, trim MSTR first.
