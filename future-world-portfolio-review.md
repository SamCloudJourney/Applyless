# Future World Portfolio: Panel Review and Verdict

Date: 2026-06-15. Method: 39 institutional expert seats (per-ETF vehicle experts,
sector reality-checks, portfolio construction, risk, tax, FX, liquidity,
geopolitics, look-through, idea scouts, implementation), web-grounded on 2026
data. Mandate held fixed: high-risk, high-conviction satellite, 10-30yr, maximum
capital gains, own the bottleneck not the narrative. Settled points not
relitigated.

## Headline verdict

Keep the architecture. It is a coherent, genuinely-distinct set of bottleneck
bets. The fixes are at the margin, not the core: cut one holding, swap one for a
purer chokepoint, add one genuine gap, and tilt weight from the narrative bets
toward the near-certain ones. Net holding count stays at 11, which the panel
agrees is at the practical limit for a ~£7,306 pot.

## The changes (high consensus)

1. CUT TKNX (5% to 0). The single weakest holding on the panel's own test. It is
   a basket of margin-compressing intermediaries (Circle, Coinbase, Block), not a
   chokepoint, two months old at ~£1.3m AUM. If the crypto bet is wanted, the
   asset is the bottleneck, not the rails: express via a spot Bitcoin ETP
   (WXBT or IB1T, commission-free on T212). Note: from 6 April 2026 crypto ETNs
   are no longer eligible for new purchase inside a Stocks ISA, so spot crypto is
   a GIA holding. Treat as a separate decision.

2. SWAP DMAT to REXC (keep ~8-9%). DMAT renamed to EART and is only ~19% rare
   earths, ~34% copper, and ~25% China, including a Chinese rare-earth producer.
   You own the chokepoint diluted and own the adversary that controls it. REXC
   (Sprott Rare Earths ex-China) is ~99% rare earths, 0% China, spanning
   mining-separation-refining-magnets, the actual weaponised bottleneck. Two
   independent seats endorsed this.

3. ADD CHPX (~8%). The one genuine missing bottleneck. The constraint of the AI
   stack has moved off the GPU onto HBM memory (sold out years forward), advanced
   packaging (CoWoS) and lithography (ASML). CHPX (Global X AI Semiconductor &
   Quantum UCITS) is HBM/equipment/packaging weighted, which avoids the generic
   mega-cap overlap that the "no generic AI" rule rightly forbids. A broad semis
   ETF (SMGB) was rejected as too Nvidia-heavy.

4. TRIM WQTM (11.1% to ~5-6%). Strongest single-holding reduction. The science is
   real and compounding, but the public pure-plays (D-Wave, Rigetti, IonQ) are
   pre-revenue, cash-burning and unlikely to capture the win even if quantum
   succeeds; the spoils favour Google/IBM/private. It is sized like a conviction
   core and should be a lottery ticket.

5. BOOST WIRE (11% to ~15%). The single most-certain multi-decade bottleneck and
   the panel's biggest underweight versus conviction. Grid, transformers and power
   electronics are the hard physical constraint on the entire AI/datacentre and
   electrification build-out (turbine/transformer backlogs of up to 4 years).

## Smaller weight moves

- TRIM URJP 8.2% to ~6%. Structural demand strong, but it is the highest-beta
  sleeve and spot has cooled mid-cycle ($101 Jan to ~$85 May 2026). Flex weight
  with the cycle: add on a junior washout, shave into euphoric spikes.
- TRIM PAIG 14% to ~13%. Theme is probable not near-certain (a back-loaded
  timeline bet on humanoid commercialisation); still earns a top-three slot, just
  not the single peak weight.
- TRIM WDNA 7.9% to ~6% and CYBO 6.9% to ~7% (roughly flat). Biorevolution is a
  perennially-early, rate-sensitive theme; the real value accrues to the
  delivery/tools layer and to acquirers.
- HOLD WCBR ~12-13%. Near-certain, non-discretionary, compounding demand; the
  most certain theme in the book alongside grid. The April 2026 cyber de-rating
  caps it but does not threaten the thesis.
- HOLD FLXI ~10-11%. The anchor. Keep it broad; Indian small-cap is ~50% above
  its valuation history and is the worse risk-reward now. Do not chase octane
  here.

## Themes ranked by structural-demand certainty (panel consensus)

Near-certain (true picks-and-shovels): electrification/grid, cybersecurity, rare
earths/critical materials, India demographics, uranium fuel demand.
Probable but timeline-dependent: physical AI/humanoids, biorevolution, medical
robotics.
Trend-might-not-materialise (lottery tickets): quantum, space, tokenisation.

## Not done, and why

- Photonics/optical interconnect is a real bottleneck, but the only UCITS vehicle
  (L&G LASR) was liquidated in early 2026; FOTO and LYTE are US-listed. Skip
  unless a US-listed ETF is acceptable in the wrapper.
- DRON (pure-play drones/autonomous defence) and NUKZ (nuclear power producers,
  owning the offtake cashflow rather than just the fuel) are both credible,
  genuinely-additive, high-risk ideas. Parked as the next-slot candidates, not
  added now: a ~£7,306 pot at 11 lines is already ~£660 per holding, and more
  lines push positions toward irrelevance.

## Risk and implementation

- Hidden concentration is a single factor, not a single stock. Roughly 70% of the
  sleeve is long-duration, rate-sensitive growth that sells off together on a
  real-yield spike or an AI-capex disappointment. That drawdown is recoverable and
  is the volatility the mandate buys. Permanent-impairment risk is narrower:
  sub-scale fund survival and single-chokepoint expropriation. Fix cheaply by
  capping lines at ~8-10% and screening out the thinnest funds, not by hedging.
- No single look-through stock exceeds ~1.2% (CrowdStrike, Palo Alto, Paladin).
  Overlap across funds is negligible. Confirmed.
- Currency: ~70-80% USD underlying. Leave unhedged over this horizon; hedging
  cost compounds against you and equity returns dwarf FX noise.
- Tax/structure: all Irish UCITS, hold the whole sleeve inside the ISA. No CGT, no
  dividend tax, no reporting-fund admin. Verify each line's UK reporting status on
  the factsheet before buying.
- Execution: use Trading 212 pie target weights. Rebalance by steering monthly
  contributions into the most underweight lines first (buy-only, free in the ISA).
  Use absolute bands (correct a line only when it drifts beyond roughly plus or
  minus 5 points); review quarterly, hard check each April. Use limit orders, not
  market orders, on the thin names (REXC, CHPX, WQTM, STRR). Biggest behavioural
  trap: panic-selling the drawdown that was the entire point.

## Dissent noted

A barbell seat argued for concentrating ~70% into PAIG/WCBR/WQTM. Accepted in
spirit (the even spread mildly closet-indexes the thesis, so weights should lean
toward conviction) but rejected in specifics: loading quantum is contradicted by
the quantum reality-check. The reweight above is the disciplined version of that
instinct.
